[ TITLE ]
Draw a Cat's Face Inside a Given Head Circle

[ PURPOSE ]
Place muzzle, eyes, nose, mouth, whiskers and lids by ratios of the head radius h, after the body agent has finished.

Start only from the body agent's HEAD_INTERFACE: head centre H = (hx, hy), radius h, facing. All offsets below are
from H in units of h, for a cat facing right; for facing left, negate every x offset. Never move or resize the head.

[ RATIOS ]
  colours       every colour comes from the CURRENT brief; never reuse a colour from an earlier cat or memory
  muzzle        white ellipse, centre (0.72h, 0.36h), rx 0.40h, ry 0.30h; it may stick out of the head circle
                at the front by up to 0.2h, which reads as the cheek and chin; the nose and mouth may reach
                0.3h past the circle and the whiskers further, as these ratios place them
  near eye      ellipse centre (0.36h, -0.06h), rx 0.19h, ry 0.15h
  far eye       ellipse centre (0.80h, -0.08h), rx 0.11h, ry 0.14h (narrower: it turns away from the viewer;
                0.02h higher than the near eye on purpose, for the head's perspective)
  pupils        vertical ellipse, rx 0.32 x eye rx, ry 0.85 x eye ry, centre shifted 0.1 x eye rx toward the face
  nose          size n = 0.10h; centre at muzzle centre + (0.92 x muzzle rx, -0.55 x muzzle ry);
                triangle (-n, -0.6n), (+0.6n, -0.6n), (0, +0.6n), pink
  mouth         a line from the nose tip down to nose y + 1.6n, then two quadratic arcs: back to -2.0n and
                forward to +0.25n, each dipping about one n, so it ends inside the muzzle; stroke 0.22n
  whiskers      three lines from muzzle centre + (0.55 x muzzle rx, 0.10 x muzzle ry) at -12, 0 and +12 degrees,
                length 1.0h, stroke 0.14n, mid grey: never white on a white muzzle or a pale sky
  blink lids    one lid per eye with the eye's own centre and size, in the eye's group, so head motion moves both

[ ALGORITHM ]
1. Read the HEAD_INTERFACE. Add a cat-face group inside cat-head.
2. Muzzle first, then far eye, near eye (each eye group holds its pupil and then its lid), nose, mouth, whiskers.
3. Keep both eyes and lids wholly inside the head circle; the muzzle, nose, mouth and whiskers may extend past it
   at the front exactly as the ratios place them. Keep the nose on the muzzle's front-top edge.
4. Print every placed centre and size from the written file and compare it with the table before closing.

[ EXAMPLES ]
[Setup]: Head centre (808, 324), h = 57.6, facing right.
[Process]: muzzle centre (849.5, 344.7), rx 23.0; near eye (828.7, 320.5) rx 10.9; nose size 5.8.
[Result]: a readable three-quarter cat face with visible whiskers inside the body agent's head circle.

[ SELF_CHECK ]
Q1: The near eye's centre is 1.1h from the head centre. Allowed?
A1: No. Both eyes stay inside the head circle; recompute from the ratios.
Q2: The whiskers pass every source check but nobody can see them. What changes?
A2: Their colour: mid grey against both the white muzzle and the background.
