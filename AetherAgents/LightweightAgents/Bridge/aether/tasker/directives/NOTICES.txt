// The Aether Timer's notices: their exact glyphs. The Overseer Directive and the staff README quote this file.
// The Timer builds every notice from the Tasker's fields; it quotes what minds wrote only as data and never judges.

∆WMC{
  WORLD_MODEL := ⌜AETHER_TIMER_NOTICES⌝

  [DEF] → ⌜FRAME⌝ := ⌞
    ⟪TIMER_NOTICE⟫ ⟪SPRINT::<sprint id>⟫ ⟪AT::<UTC time>⟫ ⟪KIND::<kind>⟫ ⟪TO::<agent id | overseer:<id>>⟫ [⟪TASK::<n>⟫]
    <blocks>
    ⟪END_TIMER_NOTICE⟫
    One notice per wake. A task is written {TASK, TITLE, STATUS, ASSIGNED_TO, DEPENDS_ON, NOTE}.
  ⌟

  [DEF] → ⌜OVERSEER_KINDS⌝ := ⌞
    HIRED        → you are the Overseer of this sprint; [QUOTE] ⌜SPRINT_GOALS⌝ follows.
    CHANGES      → [CHANGES] := [ {TASK, FIELD, FROM, TO, BY, NOTE?} , … ] and [TASKER] := {New, Active, Blocked, Closed}.
                   Every change since your last notice by staff or the operator, oldest first. A status change carries
                   the task's note as it is now. Your own changes are not sent back to you.
    QUIET        → nothing is Active and nothing can be dispatched: [BLOCKED] and [WAITING] (with why) follow.
                   One per quiet spell. Clear the blocks, add or edit tasks, or close the sprint (closure_send).
    STALE        → a task stayed Active after its turn and one re-prompt. The Timer changed nothing.
    TURN_FAILED  → a staff turn failed three times (two retries); [ERROR] := "<Type>: <message>". The task is not
                   dispatched again until you (or the operator) change it.
    REPLY        → the answer to your message_agent: [QUOTE] ⌜YOU_ASKED::<agent>⌝ and [QUOTE] ⌜REPLY::<agent>⌝.
    CLOSURE_DONE → every hired agent had its closure turn and the write-back ran: [CLOSURE] lines say what went home.
    A cap or an operator line rides on your next notice as [ASSERT] → ⌜CAP_REACHED::<cap>⌝ or ⌜OPERATOR⌝.
  ⌟

  [DEF] → ⌜STAFF_KINDS⌝ := ⌞
    TASK     → [CURRENT_TASK] ⌜T<n>⌝ and [HOW] ⌜NEXT⌝: tasker_read() first; the prompt lives in the Tasker.
               A re-prompt adds [ASSERT] ⌜STILL_ACTIVE⌝.
    Any kind, after a stop → [REMEMBER] ⌜RESUMED⌝ at its top: the same notice again; continue from where it stands.
    MESSAGE  → [QUOTE] ⌜OVERSEER_ASKS⌝ and [REMEMBER] ⌜YOUR_TASKS⌝; your final words go to the Overseer only.
    CLOSURE  → [HOW] ⌜IDENTITY_CLOSE⌝: write System 1 and one reflection; call identity_close once.
  ⌟

  [ASSERT] → ⌜THE_TIMER_NEVER⌝ := ⌞
    reads what a mind wrote to decide anything · changes a task · skips or reorders the dependency rule ·
    wakes a mind that has no reason in the table, the message queue or the closure list
  ⌟
}∆WMC
⟪END_NOTICES_DIRECTIVE⟫
