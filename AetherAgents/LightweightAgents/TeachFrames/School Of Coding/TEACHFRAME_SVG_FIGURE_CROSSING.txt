[ TITLE ]
Move a Figure Across the Scene and Repeat Cleanly

[ PURPOSE ]
Move a walking figure across stationary support with an explicit entry, exit and repeat policy.

STATUS: PILOT MODULE. This module owns outer travel and reset, not the character's drawing or
background art. It applies to a cat or another figure. Do not inject a fence into another brief.

[ ALGORITHM ]
1. Declare FIGURE_MOVES: support landmarks remain stationary, figure root moves right or left,
   and articulation occurs inside that root. Clouds or other independent scenery may still move.
   Keep travel, bob and limb transforms on separate groups unless composition is explicit.

2. Choose a repeat policy. For an ordinary rightward pass, exit fully at the right, reset fully
   off-screen at the left, and enter again. A visible jump/cut, a fade, or a turn-and-return is a
   different valid brief when deliberately chosen. Do not call a visible reset seamless.

3. Compute the full animated silhouette envelope, including tail, ears, whiskers and strokes.
   For horizontal view bounds [0,W] and local envelope [xmin,xmax], choose startX+xmax<0 and
   endX+xmin>W to hide a rightward reset; allow a margin. If scale/rotation changes the envelope,
   use the union across motion extremes. Starting with root x>=0 does not ensure full visibility;
   starting at a negative root is not itself a defect.

4. Draw the required support continuously under the visible travel route. Include enough support
   beyond the crop where needed. Declare depth so a cat does not accidentally walk through posts
   or along the rail's front face. Do not demand a particular number of rails for every fence.

5. For constant-speed travel, speed=(endX-startX)/travelPeriod. Pair it with the stance foot's
   local backward motion, using v_root+v_foot_local~=0 on static support. The distance advanced
   per full stride is speed*stridePeriod; check the geometry rather than selecting periods alone.
   Match gait phase at a visible loop seam, or hide the reset fully. A bob is not displacement.

6. Capture entry, a clear central stride, just before exit/reset, the reset instant and just after
   reentry. Check that no part teleports while still intended to be visible. Test limb phases
   separately from the much longer crossing clock. If the brief asks for immediate visibility,
   choose an initial phase or another presentation policy explicitly.

[ EXAMPLES ]
[Setup]: The supplied small cat travels from x=-60 to x=780 over 12 seconds on an 800-wide canvas.
[Process]: Its torso spans local x=-53..7, leaving torso pixels near x=727..787 just before reset.
[Result]: The cat visibly disappears at the right and later enters from the left. It demonstrates
crossing/respawn, but not a fully off-screen right exit. Preserve this attractive supplied example;
use the envelope rule when a hidden reset is required.

[ SELF_CHECK ]
Q1: Does a negative initial root position automatically fail?
A1: No. Judge the declared entry policy and the complete silhouette.
Q2: Must a 12-second travel cycle and a 0.4-second stride have different poses at 3 and 9 seconds?
A2: No. Those times are 15 strides apart; equal poses are expected.
