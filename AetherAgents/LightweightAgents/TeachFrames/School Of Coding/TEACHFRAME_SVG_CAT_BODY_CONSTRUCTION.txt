[ TITLE ]
Construct a Cat Body from Shapes, Ratios and Positions

[ PURPOSE ]
Build the cat's silhouette in a fixed order, body then head circle then ears then legs then tail, from the ratios below.

Every number is in units of R, the chest circle's radius. Pick R from the space you have, then compute everything
else. Refine style only after the construction passes its checks. You own the silhouette; the face agent owns the
eyes, nose, muzzle, mouth and whiskers and works inside the head circle you hand over.

[ COORDINATES ]
x grows right, y grows down. The cat faces right; for a cat facing left, negate every x offset. Angles on a circle
are measured counterclockwise from +x with y up: point(cx, cy, r, deg) = (cx + r*cos(deg), cy - r*sin(deg)).
"From vertical, + forward" means a segment leaning toward the face. G is the ground or support line.

[ CHOOSING_R ]
Whole cat including the tail is about 6.7R wide and 5.1R tall (ground to ear tip). Nose to rump is about 5.3R.
Example: a cat one third of a 1920-px frame (640 px with tail) has R = 640 / 6.7 = 95 px.

[ RATIOS ]
1 BODY (two circles joined by a back line and a belly line)
  chest circle      radius 1.0R, centre C = (cx, G - 2.7R)          chest bottom sits 1.7R above the ground
  hip circle        radius 0.85R, centre P = C + (-2.35R, -0.05R)
  back line         quadratic from hip-circle top to chest-circle top, control at the midpoint raised 0.12R
  belly line        quadratic from chest-circle bottom to hip-circle bottom, control at the midpoint raised 0.20R
  torso path        chest front arc (top to bottom) + belly line + hip rear arc (bottom to top) + back line
2 HEAD CIRCLE (placement only; the face agent fills it)
  head circle       radius h = 0.72R, centre H = C + (1.35R, -1.25R)
  neck back edge    from point(C, 1.0R, 105) to point(H, h, 205), bulging up 0.05R
  neck front edge   from point(H, h, 240) to point(C, 1.0R, 30), bulging out 0.05R; fill through C
3 EARS (triangles whose base sits on the head circle, inset to 0.92h so they overlap it)
  far ear           base between angles 53 and 87; apex at distance 1.70h, angle 62
  near ear          base between angles 90 and 130; apex at distance 1.80h, angle 118
  inner ear         the ear triangle scaled 0.58 toward its base midpoint, raised 0.04R, pink
4 LEGS (three segments each; anchors inside the circles; one length L solved so the paw lands on G)
  shoulder joint    C + (0.20R, 0.25R)          hip joint    P + (0.05R, 0.20R)
  fore split        upper arm 0.46L, forearm 0.41L, pastern 0.13L     widths 0.62R, 0.40R, 0.34R
  hind split        thigh 0.36L, shin 0.38L, metatarsus 0.26L         widths 1.15R, 0.42R, 0.30R
  haunch            ellipse centred 0.45 of the way down the thigh; long axis 0.70 x thigh length along the
                    thigh, short axis 0.55R; same colour as the leg
  paw               ellipse rx 0.30R, ry 0.16R, centred 0.3 x rx forward of the leg end
  neutral pose      from vertical, + forward:  fore (-26, 16, 22)   hind (24, -42, 3)
                    all four legs use the neutral pose; the walk moves them (WALK); far legs are 15-20% darker
  solve L           L = (G - 0.16R - joint_y) / sum(split_i * cos(angle_i)); expect fore 2.4-2.5R, hind 2.7-2.8R
  slack             the neutral pose must be bent: sum(split_i * cos(angle_i)) <= 0.93, or the leg cannot reach
                    the ends of its stride
  the hock          the shin angle is always negative (shin runs back and down) so the heel points backward;
                    the fore elbow points back (first angle negative)
5 TAIL (one tapered shape around a cubic centreline)
  root              point(P, 0.9 x 0.85R, 150)
  centreline        cubic: root, root + (-1.0R, -0.15R), root + (-1.8R, -1.3R), root + (-1.3R, -2.6R)
  width             0.34R at the root tapering to 0.18R, round tip; length about 3.3R
6 MARKINGS (when the brief asks for them; colours come from the brief)
  white chest       ellipse centre C + (0.80R, -0.30R), rx 0.42R, ry 0.75R, clipped to the torso and neck shapes
  white socks       the lower 40% of each leg's last segment plus the paw
  tail rings        bands across the tail at 25%, 45%, 65% and 85% of its length, each 0.08 of the length wide

[ ALGORITHM ]
1. Choose R and G. Compute C, P and draw the two circles as construction guides (not shipped).
2. Build the torso path from the two arcs, the back line and the belly line.
3. Place the head circle H and the neck edges. Publish the HEAD INTERFACE before the face agent starts.
4. Place both ears from their angles; inset bases into the head circle; add the inner ears.
5. For each leg compute the joint anchor, solve L for its pose angles, place joints segment by segment, draw each
   segment as a tapered quad with a round cap at its lower joint, then the paw; add the haunch to hind legs.
6. Build the tail polygon from the sampled centreline and its normals; round the tip.
7. Paint in depth order: tail, far legs (15-20% darker), torso, neck, near legs, ears, head circle. Group each part
   with its own pivot: a translate group at the joint holding the rotating group, so animation never replaces the
   placement transform or moves the construction.
8. Animate with the WALK steps. Compute every coordinate and keyframe list in ONE inline Python program from the
   RATIOS and WALK numbers, then write the SVG from its output; do not type geometry by hand.
9. Run SELF_CHECK on the numbers you printed from the written file. Then hand over to the face agent.
10. Colours come from the current brief, never from an earlier cat. Whoever places the cat into a scene keeps the
   cat's own title and desc.

[ GAIT_LIMITS ]
Choose ground speed from the legs, never the legs from a speed. Stance travel S = v * duty * T (v ground speed,
T stride period). Require S <= 0.62 * L_fore (the shorter leg). If a given v breaks it, lengthen T only within
1.0-1.6 s, lower duty only within 0.55-0.65, or enlarge R; otherwise report the conflict. The lead's interface states
R and L before it states a speed.

[ WALK ]
A walk is a paw path per leg plus two-bone inverse kinematics, emitted as rotate keyframes in nested joint groups.
1 Clock       T = stride period, duty 0.6, phase offsets near hind 0, near fore 0.25, far hind 0.5, far fore 0.75.
              u = (t / T + phase) mod 1. N keyframes per stride with N * duty and N * 0.25 whole numbers (N = 40).
2 Body bob    the whole body group moves y = -0.015R * cos(4 * pi * t / T). The legs' paw paths include this bob.
3 Paw path    relative to the leg's top joint; x0 = neutral paw-end x; ground_y = G - 0.16R - (joint_y + bob).
              stance (u < duty):  x = x0 + S/2 - S * u / duty;            y = ground_y
              swing  (u >= duty): s = (u - duty) / (1 - duty);  x = x0 - S/2 + S * (3s^2 - 2s^3);
                                  y = ground_y - lift * sin(pi * s);  lift fore 0.35R, hind 0.30R
4 Last joint  a3 = a3_neutral + tilt * (x - x0) / (S/2), minus flex * sin(pi * s) during swing only;
              tilt fore 25, hind 15 degrees; flex fore 40, hind 15 degrees
5 IK          target for the two upper segments: q = paw end - l3 * (sin a3, cos a3); d = min(|q|, 0.999 (l1 + l2));
              phi = atan2(qx, qy); a = acos((l1^2 + d^2 - l2^2) / (2 l1 d));
              a1 = phi + a for a hind leg (knee forward), phi - a for a fore leg (elbow back);
              knee = l1 * (sin a1, cos a1); a2 = atan2(qx - knee_x, qy - knee_y). All angles from vertical, + forward.
6 Emit        each segment is drawn pointing down its own local +y and nested:
              <g transform="translate(joint)"><g><animateTransform type="rotate" values="-a1 ..."/> upper
                <g transform="translate(0 l1)"><g><animateTransform type="rotate" values="-(a2-a1) ..."/> middle
                  <g transform="translate(0 l2)"><g><animateTransform type="rotate" values="-(a3-a2) ..."/> last, paw
              (SVG rotate turns clockwise, so a forward lean is a negative value for a right-facing cat.)
7 Tail        rotate the tail group about its root by +/-6 degrees over 2T.
8 Check       recompute the paw from the emitted keyframes at 8 points between each pair: stance paws within
              1 px of the ground and moving back at v within 5%. Print both numbers, and the tail sway
              amplitude (it must be +/-6 degrees), before closing.

[ HEAD_INTERFACE ]
Publish for the face agent: head centre H, head radius h, facing (right or left), the group id holding the head
(cat-head) and the ear apexes. The face agent adds a cat-face group inside cat-head and never moves or resizes the
head circle. Blink lids belong to the face agent.

[ EXAMPLES ]
[Setup]: R = 80, G = 640, cx = 700, facing right.
[Process]: C = (700, 424); P = (512, 420); H = (808, 324), h = 57.6; fore L solves to 2.47R, hind 2.74R.
[Result]: nose to rump 5.33R, shoulder height 3.7R: renders as a cat, not a tube on stilts. At R = 95, v = 200 px/s,
T = 1.2 s, duty 0.6: S = 144 px = 0.61 L_fore; the WALK steps keep stance paws within 0.2 px of the ground.

[ SELF_CHECK ]
Q1: Nose to rump is 7R and the legs are 1.5R. What went wrong?
A1: The hip circle moved too far back or the legs were drawn before R was fixed; return to the RATIOS.
Q2: The brief's speed needs a stance travel of 0.95L. Draw longer strides?
A2: No. Report it; the speed must come from the legs (GAIT_LIMITS), or the legs get longer by ratio.
Q3: Can I check the silhouette without seeing it?
A3: Partly: print the measured ratios from the written file and compare with the table; appearance stays
UNVERIFIED until someone renders it.
